### 3. The conversation layer: `POST /say`, generated but constrained (`6856ee5`)
- **Shape:** `POST /say { intent, data, lang }` with intents `ask`, `confirm`, `abstain`, `readRoute`, `comfort`, `found`, `emergency`.
- **Provider-agnostic:** `LLM_API_KEY` secret, `LLM_PROVIDER` / `LLM_MODEL` in `wrangler.toml`.
  - `anthropic`: the default, model `claude-opus-5-5`, plain fetch, effort low, server-side refusal fallback;
  - `openai-compatible`: any `/chat/completions` API.
- **The checker** (`replies.js` `checkReply`) runs on the proxy **and again on the phone**. It requires:
  - numbers, places and directions only from `data`;
  - no medical advice, no "where they are", no water / roads / property, no promises, no "chatbot", in 5 languages;
  - one line, at most 2 sentences, a length cap, and the right language.
- **Fallback:** any failure, no key, no signal → the fixed template, in all 5 languages.
- **Fixed intents:** `emergency` and `found` never reach the LLM.
- **Warnings** (water, vehicle) are fixed lines added after the check.
- **Tests:**
  - an adversarial fake LLM: **0 of 720** bad replies got through;
  - fuzzing: 500 of 500 planted errors rejected;
  - 12 good paraphrases pass;
  - a tampered proxy is caught by the phone.
- **The screen hook-up list** is in `docs/REPLIES.md`: 6 steps, `app.js` / `index.html` / `i18n.js` / `style.css` / `sw.js` / deploy.
